## [0.1.0] — 2026-08-14

Initial vertical. Phase 1 factory proof.

### Added — canonical model

- Entity types `manufacturer`, `equipment_model`, `certification`.
  `certification` is a first-class entity rather than attributes on the model, so
  a certified rating and a manufacturer-published rating can coexist as distinct
  evidenced claims and a certification's own lifecycle stays answerable.
- Alias types `model_number`, `ahri_ref`, `manufacturer_sku` (scoped to
  manufacturer), `upc`, `series_name` (weak).
- Relationship predicates `manufactures`, `supersedes` (transitive),
  `certified_by`, `compatible_with` (symmetric, inference forbidden).
- 12 properties on `equipment_model`; 6 marked critical.
- 11 declarative quality rules on `equipment_model`, 2 on `manufacturer`, 3 on
  `certification`, and 6 graph integrity rules.

## Proposed vocabulary additions

Recorded rather than added, so the reasoning survives the next time someone asks.

### `lifecycle_status` on `equipment_model` — **rejected for now**

`acme-hvac-catalog` publishes `lifecycle.status` (`active` / `discontinued`) and
`lifecycle.discontinued_on`. Neither is ingested.

The graph already encodes both: a model with an inbound `supersedes` edge is
superseded, and the date lives on that edge's `valid_from`. A denormalized
property would duplicate the edge and could drift out of agreement with it —
and when a status flag and a graph edge disagree, neither can be trusted.

Reconsider only if query patterns show users filtering on lifecycle without
caring about the replacement, which would make the graph walk pure overhead.

### `frequency_hz` — **rejected**

Parsed out of the electrical notation and discarded. Every unit in scope is
60 Hz, and a property with one value cannot discriminate anything. It would add
a fact to every entity that no query could ever use.

### `mini_split` as a `product_type` term — **rejected for now**

Ductless systems are a genuinely different product class with different identity
and compatibility rules. Mapping them onto `air_conditioner` would place
incomparable equipment in the same filter. They need their own canonical term
when the vertical expands — a `schema_version` bump, not a synonym.

### Change discipline for this vertical

- **Identifier normalization changes are always breaking.** The rules in
  `normalizers/03-domain-normalization.yaml` produce every join key; changing one
  re-keys the entire vertical. Bump `schema_version`, note it here, re-verify the
  goldens.
- **Golden files change only when the canonical model changes** — never to make
  a failing test pass. Record the reason here.
- **Indexability thresholds are policy.** Lowering one publishes thinner pages;
  it needs a reason in this file, not a quiet edit.
