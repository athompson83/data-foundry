- Canonical capability hostnames are `data.aroqon.com`, `api.data.aroqon.com`, and `mcp.data.aroqon.com`; verticals belong in paths/configuration rather than long-term per-industry infrastructure hostnames.
- Do not authorize public DNS cutover, source activation, legal acceptance, credential exposure, or payment changes through this documentation work.
- Do not delay UA-002 or the first paid machine-data release.
