# Research round 1 — 2026-09-27

Six candidates were evaluated in parallel against live sources from this egress,
with polite requests and a declared User-Agent (with contact, for SEC). Quoted
terms, counts and hit rates were measured on 2026-09-27. Competitor notes are
from memory and are marked as such. The ranking is a judgement applied to that
evidence.

Drug labels tie clinical trials on raw score but rank below it. Onboarding is
L rather than M, sponsor copyright blocks verbatim text, and a missed
interaction carries higher liability. Clinical trials is the purest example of
"unstructured → structured for agents": a median of 16 free-text bullets per
study becomes typed criteria.

## 1. ClinicalTrials.gov eligibility (AMBER)

**Terms** (clinicaltrials.gov/about-site/terms-conditions, read from the site
bundle):
- attribute ClinicalTrials.gov;
- "update the data such that they are current at all times";
- display the processing date;
- state our modifications;
- "shall not assert any proprietary rights";
- no marketing use of emails;
- international copyright outside the US, and possible third-party copyright.

**robots.txt** still disallows `/api/` for crawlers. NLM's API page and its July
2025 Technical Bulletin direct programmatic users to the v2 API, so the plan is
the documented bulk download (`/api/v2/studies/download?format=json.zip`) plus
at most 1 request/second daily deltas.

**Volume:** 604,566 studies; 65,044 recruiting; data refreshed weekdays
(`dataTimestamp`). Contacts contain personal data and are excluded.

**Structure measured** (120 recruiting interventional studies):
- criteria median 1,805 characters, 16 bullets;
- Inclusion/Exclusion headings 95%, bullets 97%;
- age 58%, pregnancy 49%, prior therapy 41%, ECOG 26%, HIV/HBV/HCV 25%, lab
  thresholds 20% (e.g. `eGFR ≥ 40`, `ANC <=750`).

**Agent questions:** "recruiting NSCLC trials near Boston accepting ECOG 2 and
prior immunotherapy"; "why is this patient excluded", answered with the cited
bullet.

## 2. CPSC recalls (GREEN)

**Terms:** CPSC says web text is "public information. You may freely distribute,
copy…"; credit CPSC and imply no endorsement. The data.gov licence is public
domain.

**Volume:** 10,027 recalls from one unauthenticated call.

**Structure measured:**
- the structured `Model` and `HazardType` fields are empty in all 2,276 recalls
  since 2020;
- model numbers appear in the prose of 1,183 of those recalls;
- in 40 samples: lot/serial/date codes 14/40, sale date ranges 39/40, hazard
  keywords 39/40.

`parseCodes` and `parseQuantity` from `packages/recall-structuring` carry over.
`StructuredRecall` needs generalising beyond openFDA categories.

**Notes:**
- Seven or more Apify actors already resell raw unified recall feeds; our edge is
  the identifiers parsed out of the prose, plus provenance.
- The NHTSA flat file moved to `FLAT_RCL_POST_2010.zip`; `FLAT_RCL.zip` is 404.
- NHTSA is already structured, so it adds coverage, not structuring value.

## 3. Federal Register + eCFR (GREEN)

**Terms:** GovInfo says US government works carry no copyright. Standards
incorporated by reference remain third-party copyright and are not republished.

**Acquisition:** the API is open, with no key. Over the last 12 months: 2,719
rules and 1,645 proposed rules. Full text comes from GovInfo (35 of 35 fetched).

**The gap:** stay chains and compliance dates live only in prose. Example,
2026-17979: "compliance date … stayed … further stayed until September 1, 2026
… is further stayed until March 1…".

## 4. Drug label facts (AMBER)

**Terms:** openFDA is CC0 but carves out "copyrightable works made available to
the FDA by private entities". NLM requires permission for private content.

**Measured:** 14 of 100 prescription labels carry sponsor "All rights reserved"
notices, mostly in medication guides and patient package inserts.

**Volume:** 262,887 labels; 36,610 prescription.

**Hit rates:**
- dose strengths 91%;
- interaction actions 77%;
- pregnancy/lactation Risk Summary 70%;
- boxed-warning heading 78% of the labels that have one.

**Product shape:** facts plus evidence spans plus a DailyMed link, with no
verbatim bulk text.

## 5. SEC 8-K events (GREEN)

**Terms** (https://www.sec.gov/about/privacy-information): "Information presented
on sec.gov is considered public information and may be copied or further
distributed by users of the web site without the SEC's permission." The same
page forbids use of the SEC seal and logos, and forbids "SEC" or "EDGAR" in a
trade name, trademark or domain name without a licence.

**Fair access** (https://www.sec.gov/about/developer-resources and
https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data):
- "no more than 10 requests per second";
- no "unclassified" bots;
- a declared User-Agent with a contact.

Filing text is written by the filer, so we publish extracted facts and links,
not bulk exhibits.

**Volume:** the quarterly `form.idx` shows 14,649 8-Ks for Q3 2026 to Sep 26,
about 58,000 a year. Items are already tagged in the submissions JSON.

**Samples:** 20 live filings, 4 for each of Items 1.01, 1.03, 1.05, 4.01 and
5.02. The script and samples are in the session scratchpad, reproducible from
`data.sec.gov/submissions/` and `efts.sec.gov`.

| Item | Hit rates |
| --- | --- |
| 1.03 | case number 4/4, court 3/4, chapter 3/4, DIP 3/4 |
| 1.05 | discovery date 4/4, materiality language 4/4 |
| 4.01 | new/former auditor and dismissal/resignation 2–3/4 |
| 1.01 | agreement type/amount/date 4/4, but counterparty and amount were often wrong |
| 5.02 | role/event/effective date 2/4; names produced false positives |

Four samples per item is enough to decide the build order, not enough for a
precision claim. A 1.05/1.03/4.01 prototype must measure precision over at least
100 filings before it advances.

**Incumbent (from memory):** sec-api.io.
