Transport Canada Recall - 2026433 - FIAT CHRYSLER AUTOMOBILES

Brand(s)
JEEP
Last updated
2026-09-25

Summary

Product
SUV recalled by FIAT CHRYSLER AUTOMOBILES
Issue
Vehicle - Accessories
What to do

Please see corrective actions section below

Affected products

2022 | JEEP | WAGONEER, 2022 | JEEP | GRAND WAGONEER, 2023 | JEEP | GRAND WAGONEER, 2023 | JEEP | WAGONEER, 2024 | JEEP | GRAND WAGONEER, 2024 | JEEP | WAGONEER, 2025 | JEEP | WAGONEER, 2025 | JEEP | GRAND WAGONEER, 2026 | JEEP | GRAND WAGONEER

Issue

On certain vehicles, the pivot bolt(s) securing the roof crossbars may not have been tightened properly. As a result, the crossbar(s) could detach from the vehicle. Note: This recall also affects certain 2023-2026 Wagoneer L models and Grand Wagoneer L models.

Safety risk

A crossbar that detaches from the vehicle could create a road hazard for others and increase the risk of a crash.

Corrective actions

FCA Canada will notify owners in writing and advise you to take your vehicle to a dealership to inspect and secure the roof crossbar(s), as necessary.

Background

Visit Transport Canada’s Motor Vehicle Safety Recalls Database for the original recall.
