PERMA-HAND™ Silk Suture

Brand(s)
Ethicon LLC
Last updated
2026-09-25

Summary

Product
PERMA-HAND™ Silk Suture
Issue
Medical devices - Performance
What to do

Contact the manufacturer if you require additional information.

Affected products

Affected products

Lot or serial number

Model or catalogue number

PERMA-HAND™ Silk Suture

RLBDPJ

N271H

Issue

Ethicon determined that PERMA-HAND™ Silk Suture subject to this recall (removal) was incorrectly manufactured with an alcohol-based tubing fluid in the primary packaging. This tubing fluid is an isopropyl alcohol (IPA), diethylaminoethanol (DEAE), and sodium benzoate solution previously used in the manufacture of a different suture product. Up to 80% of the impacted lot may have had the alcohol-based tubing fluid. Tubing fluid is not a material approved for use in the manufacture of PERMA-HAND™ Silk Suture.
