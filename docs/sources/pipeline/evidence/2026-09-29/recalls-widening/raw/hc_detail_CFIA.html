Smarter Snacks brand Vegan Protein Puff Chili & Lime Flavored contains undeclared milk

Issue

The Canadian Food Inspection Agency (CFIA) is warning people who are allergic or sensitive to the affected product not to consume the affected product because it contains milk which is not declared on the label.

What you should do

  • Check to see if you have affected products
  • Do not consume affected products to which you are allergic or sensitive as they may cause a serious or life-threatening reaction
  • Do not serve, use, sell or distribute affected products

Learn more:

  • Learn more about common food allergies
  • Sign up for recall notifications by email and follow us on social media

  • Report a food safety or labelling concern

Related recalls and alerts

  • Smarter Snacks brand Vegan Protein Puff Chili & Lime Flavored recalled due to undeclared milk

Additional information

Background

This warning was triggered by Canadian Food Inspection Agency test results.

There have been no reported reactions associated with the consumption of this product.

What is being done

The Canadian Food Inspection Agency (CFIA) is conducting a food safety investigation, which may lead to the recall of this or other products.
