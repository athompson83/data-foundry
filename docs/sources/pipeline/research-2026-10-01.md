## equipment-energystar-model-index

- **Source.** EPA ENERGY STAR Certified Products Model Index, `https://data.energystar.gov/resource/8wj2-sec8.json`
  (Socrata open-data API; no login, key or CAPTCHA). The UPC Codes dataset is
  `https://data.energystar.gov/resource/8edu-y555.json`. Both were read in pages of 200,000 rows ordered by `pd_id`.
- **Size and freshness.** 1,796,876 models in 47 categories, all `pd_id` distinct, and 39,108 UPC rows. The API
  reported the dataset updated on 2026-09-30.
- **Terms, verbatim.** From <https://edg.epa.gov/EPA_Data_License.html> (retrieved 2026-10-01, SHA-256
  `deff40a15da6593a56f4c3bf4a975b1430c809635d382da95cb9dbd3da4ef052`, unchanged since 2026-09-30):

  > Unless otherwise specified, all data produced by the U.S EPA is by default in the public domain and is not subject to domestic copyright protection under 17 U.S.C. § 105.

  The dataset metadata at <https://data.energystar.gov/api/views/8wj2-sec8.json> names the licence:
  `"License": "https://edg.epa.gov/EPA_Data_License.html" (metadata.custom_fields Common Core, Publisher U.S. Environmental Protection Agency)`.
- **Deterministic hit rates** (full population, `scripts/energystar_assess.py`):

  | Rule | Hits |
  | --- | --- |
  | brand present | 1,796,876 / 1,796,876 |
  | model number with letters or digits | 1,796,875 / 1,796,876 |
  | model filed as a pattern (`*`, `?`, `#`) | 1,576,700 / 1,796,876 |
  | at least one check-digit-valid GTIN (own `upc` plus UPC Codes) | 11,719 / 1,796,876 |
  | a UPC that fails the check digit (kept for audit, never linked) | 886 / 1,796,876 |
  | at least one known market / Canada listed | 1,796,875 / 1,644,779 |
  | certification and on-market dates parse | 1,796,876 / 1,796,876 |

- **Sample.** 25 rows, every 71,875th by `pd_id` (listed in `results/energystar_assess.txt`). 24 are Central AC / Heat
  Pump listings and 22 are filed as patterns. This mirrors the population: that category is 97.5% of rows.
