## Time zone rules (`time-zone-rules`, prefix `tz`, category reference): SCREENED

Entity: one time zone, keyed by its IANA tz identifier (America/New_York), with its UTC offset and daylight-saving rule history, country mapping, aliases and links, CLDR canonical id, Windows zone and metazone, and the dated rule changes announced in release prose. Script: `evidence/2026-11-20/tz_measure.py` (13 requests) -> `tz_measure.json`; hand-check `tz_handcheck.json`. No existing candidate covers these hosts' time zone data.

**IANA tz database, `tz-iana-tzdb`, structured, GREEN (public domain), SCREENED.** `data.iana.org/time-zones/tzdb/` serves the current release unpacked: version 2026f (NEWS dates it 2026-10-27). `tzdata.zi` holds 345 Zones and 253 Links (598 identifiers; every Link target is a Zone), 131 named rule sets in 1,974 rule lines. Zones by area: America 121, Asia 74, Europe 38, Pacific 30, Etc 28, Africa 19, Australia 11, Antarctica 8, Atlantic 8, Indian 3, and 5 legacy ids without an area. `zone1970.tab`: 312 rows, all Zones, over 247 country codes, 34 rows shared by several countries. `zone.tab`: 418 rows, all resolving, 106 of them Links. The release directory lists 249 data tarballs from 1996l to 2026f; NEWS counts 6 releases in 2026, 3 in 2025, 2 in 2024, 4 in 2023, 7 in 2022.

**tzdb NEWS and source commentary, `tz-iana-tzdb-news-commentary`, free-text, AMBER, SCREENED.** `NEWS` (264,231 bytes) has 314 release blocks from 1992-04-25 to 2026f; the median block is 178 characters because the early ones are one-liners, while 39 releases carry a "Changes to future timestamps" section and 34 a "Changes to past timestamps" section. 45 releases open with a "Briefly" summary. 68 releases cite at least one tz identifier in prose (26 of the newest 40). The 2026f data tarball (`tzdata-latest.tar.gz`, 483,310 bytes, SHA-256 `01b4a91c…4d3d`, 32 files) carries the per-region source files, whose comments are the longer free text: 14,381 comment lines, 726,469 characters (asia 158,497; northamerica 148,485; europe 144,355), including 1,277 attributed "From" lines that quote correspondents and press reports. Same publisher as the structured member, and public domain by the same statement; AMBER only because of the quoted third-party passages and the names of people, which stay out of every surface.

**Unicode CLDR time zone data, `tz-unicode-cldr-timezones`, structured, AMBER (Unicode License V3), SCREENED.** Read from the `main` branch of `unicode-org/cldr` on raw.githubusercontent.com (cldr-json core reports 49.0.0). `common/bcp47/timezone.xml`: 469 tz types, 18 deprecated, 451 with aliases, 601 distinct alias identifiers. `common/supplemental/windowsZones.xml`: 505 mapZone rows for 142 Windows zone names over 445 distinct tz identifiers (the file declares `typeVersion` 2021a). `common/supplemental/metaZones.xml`: 419 time zones with a metazone history, 155 metazones, 283 metazone-to-zone rows. The licence permits sale and redistribution provided the notice travels with the data; the LDML specification text in the same repository is excluded from that licence and is not taken.

**Not a member:** the tz-announce mailing-list archive (`lists.iana.org/hyperkitty/list/tz-announce@iana.org/` answered 200, not parsed; same publisher). Government legal-time notices (national decrees, the EU summer-time directive) and the Microsoft time zone update notes were not probed. timeanddate.com is a commercial site and was not considered.

**Joins.** All on the IANA time zone identifier, which names the counterpart record.
- Declared, CLDR BCP 47 alias -> tzdb: 598/601 distinct alias identifiers resolve to a Zone (345) or Link (253); the 3 others are Etc/Unknown, US/Pacific-New and Canada/East-Saskatchewan. In the other direction all 598 tzdb identifiers are CLDR aliases.
- Declared, CLDR Windows zone mapping -> tzdb: 445/445 (324 Zones, 121 Links).
- Declared, CLDR metazone timezone -> tzdb: 419/419 (298 Zones, 121 Links); 298 of the 345 tzdb Zones have a metazone history, and all 280 distinct metazone mapZone identifiers resolve.
- Canonical forms differ: of CLDR's 451 canonical identifiers (the first alias of a type), 329 are tzdb Zones and 121 are tzdb Links (Asia/Calcutta, America/Buenos_Aires, Africa/Accra ...), so a consumer needs both publishers' views to translate.
- Declared, identifier cited in NEWS prose -> tzdb: 323/327 distinct cited identifiers resolve (not resolving: America/Honolulu, America/New_York/Bronx, Australia/Macquarie, Etc/Unknown); same publisher. Against CLDR, 323/323 of the resolving ones are aliases.
- Candidate, place word in a "Briefly" summary = city part of a Zone identifier (5+ letters): 11/45 summaries, 16 release-zone pairs. All 16 were read: 13 correct, 3 wrong ("The northern edge of Chihuahua" concerns other zones; "switches to Moscow time" names Moscow only as the reference; TZ="EST5EDT" is a POSIX setting). Place words only propose a zone.

**Outcome:** dataset `time-zone-rules` SCREENED with 3 members from 2 publishers (2 structured, 1 free-text); nothing parked. No allow-list change is needed: the declared-key pattern already accepts `zone`. Caveats: the free-text member shares its publisher with tzdb, so the independent pair is IANA with Unicode (both structured) and an independent free-text member is still to be found; CLDR mirrors tzdb identifiers by design, so the joins show coverage and canonical-form differences rather than independent confirmation; existing free access is very good (every operating system ships tzdb), so the value lies in the dated, explained change events and the joined mappings, not in access.
