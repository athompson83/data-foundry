**tzdb NEWS and source commentary, `tz-iana-tzdb-news-commentary`, free-text, AMBER, SCREENED.** `NEWS` (264,231 bytes) has 314 release blocks from 1992-04-25 to 2026f; the median block is 178 characters because the early ones are one-liners, while 39 releases carry a "Changes to future timestamps" section and 34 a "Changes to past timestamps" section. 45 releases open with a "Briefly" summary. 68 releases cite at least one tz identifier in prose (26 of the newest 40). **Unicode CLDR time zone data, `tz-unicode-cldr-timezones`, structured, AMBER (Unicode License V3), SCREENED.** Read from the `main` branch of `unicode-org/cldr` on raw.githubusercontent.com (cldr-json core reports 49.0.0). `common/bcp47/timezone.xml`: 469 tz types, 18 deprecated, 451 with aliases, 601 distinct alias identifiers. `common/supplemental/windowsZones.xml`: 505 mapZone rows for 142 Windows zone names over 445 distinct tz identifiers (the file declares `typeVersion` 2021a). `common/supplemental/metaZones.xml`: 419 time zones with a metazone history, 155 metazones, 283 metazone-to-zone rows. - Canonical forms differ: of CLDR's 451 canonical identifiers (the first alias of a type), 329 are tzdb Zones and 121 are tzdb Links (Asia/Calcutta, America/Buenos_Aires, Africa/Accra ...), so a consumer needs both publishers' views to translate.
